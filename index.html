How it plays

Fight without the grind

Set your hero loose on a trail of meadows, forests, caves and castles. They keep fighting while the app is closed, and every session leaves them better off.

Auto-battle

45 stages across five biomes, each with its own monsters and bosses. Tap in only when you want to.

Skill rotations

Learn attacks along the Blade, Arcane and Guard paths, then chain them: a chill sets up a shatter, an expose sets up a crit.

Mini-games that pay

Plinko, Block Stack and Match-3 pay XP and gold. Battles pay gear and gems. Both loops stay worth your time.

Come back to a chest

An idle chest fills for hours. Every loss refunds its energy. The fix is levels and gear, not retries.

Mini-games

Thirty seconds or thirty minutes

Quick arcade rounds between battles, each one paying into the same hero.

  • Plinko. Fire 20 balls down a cabinet of pegs, chase the XP multiplier and the mystery pocket.
  • Block Stack. Drop blocks clean for perfects and a taller tower.
  • Match-3. Clear boards to climb the ladder.

FAQ

Good to know

Is Evergreen free?

Yes. Evergreen is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases sell gems, a one-time Starter Bundle and a Monthly Card. None of them are required to finish the game.

Does the gacha show its odds?

The banner in the app shows its Legendary guarantee: every pull on the standard banner counts toward a guaranteed Legendary within 50 pulls. Reward odds are available in the app or from us on request.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login. If you sign in to Game Center, your progress is backed up to iCloud and restored on your other devices. The game is fully playable without it.

Can I cash out Plinko or gacha winnings?

No. Plinko and the mini-games pay only in-game gold and XP, and gacha rewards are in-game items. Nothing in Evergreen can be exchanged for real money.

What platforms is it on?

iPhone and iPad first. An Android release is planned; follow this page for news.

How do I restore purchases or reset progress?

Both live in Settings, the gear icon at the top right of the hub. Restore Purchases re-grants the Starter Bundle and any active Monthly Card. Reset Progress deletes your local save and its cloud backup.
